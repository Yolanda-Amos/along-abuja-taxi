Along!Berger to Wuse

You drive a green-and-white shared taxi through Abuja. Fill your four seats, drop each passenger at their stop and earn the fare.

  • Switch lanes with ← → (or A / D), or tap the left or right side of the screen.
  • Hold STOP (Space or ↓) in the left lane at a bus stop to load and drop passengers.
  • Miss a passenger's stop and you earn nothing from them. Chain good stops for a combo.
  • An unpainted car lurking at a stop? HONK (H) to warn the passengers.
Real-life tip: board only painted, registered green-and-white taxis.
